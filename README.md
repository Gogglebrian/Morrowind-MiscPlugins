Various simple esp mods for Morrowind/OpenMW:
- TimsaWeightFix - Fixes Timsa-Come-By flowers' Weight from 1.0 to 0.1
- blisteringspeed - The Boots of Blinding Speed become the Boots of Blistering Speed, trading in the Blind effect for Drain Agility and Drain Willpower, 50 points each.
- expensivecustomenchants - Doubles the cost of Enchanting services.
- exquisiteclothesplus - Adds higher-enchant pants, shirt, skirt, belt, amulet (available at weights 1-12), and ring via the console.
  - For example: "player->additem exquisite_pants_plus 1" or "player->additem exquisite_amulet_plus_7 1"
  - Use with Transmog Menu to combine multiple CE enchants into one and ditch ugly robes or skirts.
- patchForPuristsFourCornersPilgrimage - reverts Patch for Purists's "fix" of the Four Corners pilgrimage sequence break.
- Higher training costs x4 - Quadruples the cost for skill training.
